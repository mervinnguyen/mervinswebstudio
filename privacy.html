This policy explains what Mervin's Web Studio collects when you visit this website or contact me about a project.

This site may use privacy-friendly analytics after an analytics provider is configured. If enabled, analytics may collect anonymous page views, referring pages, device type, and approximate location. The site does not use advertising cookies or sell personal information.

Service providers

Third-party providers may process information on my behalf when they host the site, deliver an inquiry form, or provide analytics. They are used only for the services needed to operate the website.

Data retention and requests

Inquiry information is kept only as long as reasonably necessary to respond and manage a potential client relationship. To ask about, correct, or delete information associated with an inquiry, email mervinnguyen@mervinsmerchandise.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website or its providers change. The date above indicates when it was last revised.
